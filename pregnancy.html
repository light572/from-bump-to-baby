Pregnancy

Preparing your body, mind and support system.

Understanding pregnancy

Pregnancy brings physical, emotional and practical changes. Antenatal care helps monitor progress, identify risks and prepare for birth.

Healthy preparation

  • Keep antenatal appointments and records together.
  • Take prescribed supplements as advised.
  • Ask before taking medicines or herbal products.
  • Plan transport for routine care and emergencies.
  • Discuss who can support you after birth.
Remember

Every pregnancy is different. Your healthcare professional can give advice specific to your pregnancy.
