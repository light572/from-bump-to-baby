LEARN & PREPARE

Pregnancy

Build a support plan, prepare for appointments and talk with your healthcare team about what to expect.

Antenatal care

Attend recommended antenatal appointments and bring questions or concerns. Your healthcare team can advise you based on your pregnancy and health history.

Prepare for birth

  • Keep maternity records and identification accessible.
  • Plan transport to your chosen maternity facility.
  • Discuss your birth preferences with your care team.
  • Ask who to contact if symptoms concern you.
  • Arrange support for recovery and newborn care.

Medicines and supplements

Use medicines and supplements as advised by your qualified healthcare professional. Check before taking new medicines or herbal products.

Birth plans

A birth plan is a communication tool, not a guarantee. Plans may change when the clinical situation changes.

Questions to ask

What should I expect at my next visit? Which symptoms mean I should seek care urgently? Where should I go after hours?
