Newborn Warning Signs

Know what deserves prompt professional attention.

Breathing difficulty
Blue or grey colour
Unresponsive or unusually difficult to wake
Repeated refusal of feeds
Seizure or abnormal movements
Concerning vomiting or diarrhoea
Signs of dehydration
Rapid worsening or serious illness

🚨 Emergency

Seek emergency medical help immediately for severe or rapidly worsening symptoms.
