SAFETY FIRST

Warning signs

This is an awareness guide, not a diagnostic checklist. When in doubt, contact a qualified health professional promptly.

Difficulty breathing or severe struggle to breathe
Blue or grey colour, collapse or unresponsiveness
Seizure or unusual repeated movements
Baby is very difficult to wake or unusually weak
Repeated refusal of feeds or significant feeding difficulty
Fever or unusually low temperature in a newborn
Rapid worsening or a caregiver's strong concern that something is wrong
Any urgent warning sign identified by your healthcare team
Act early.

For severe or rapidly worsening symptoms, seek emergency medical care immediately. Do not wait for a website or WhatsApp response.
