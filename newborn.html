PART 3

Newborn Care

Warmth, feeding, observation and connection.

The first hours

Newborns may receive assessment of breathing, temperature, circulation and feeding, with skin-to-skin contact when appropriate.

Feeding

Learn early hunger cues and seek skilled support when feeding is difficult.

Cord care

Keep the cord clean and dry and seek care for concerning redness, swelling, pus, foul smell or significant bleeding.

Safe sleep

Place baby on the back on a firm, flat sleep surface. Keep pillows, loose blankets and soft objects out of the sleep area.

Normal behaviour

Frequent feeding, sleeping, crying, hiccups and sneezing can occur. A baby who is difficult to wake or feeds poorly needs prompt assessment.
