THE FIRST DAYS

Newborn care

Gentle, practical topics to discuss with your maternity or child-health team.

Feeding

Seek skilled support if feeding is painful, difficult, or your baby repeatedly refuses feeds. Ask how to recognise effective feeding and when to return for review.

Warmth

Keep your baby comfortably warm and follow your care team's guidance, especially in the first hours and days.

Cord care

Follow local clinical advice and keep the cord clean and dry. Seek professional advice about redness, swelling, pus, foul smell or bleeding.

Safer sleep

Place baby on their back on a firm, flat sleep surface. Keep pillows, loose bedding and soft objects out of the baby's sleep space.

Follow-up

Before leaving the maternity facility, confirm the follow-up plan, feeding support and who to contact if you are worried.

Bonding

Comfort, gentle touch and responding to your baby's cues can support bonding. Ask for help if you feel overwhelmed.
