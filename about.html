OUR PURPOSE

About From Bump to Baby

A phone-friendly maternal and newborn education platform built around the educational vision of Nurse Kerubo Nyakundi.

The vision

Make helpful learning resources easier for parents and caregivers to access, revisit and use when preparing questions for their healthcare team.

What you'll find here

Educational topics, checklists, a simple journal demo, book information and a way to enquire about consultations.

What this platform is not

It does not diagnose, replace physical assessment, provide emergency response or replace individualized care from a qualified professional.

Designed for mobile

The site is a static front-end. A production version with appointments, payments or client records will need a secure backend and appropriate privacy safeguards.
