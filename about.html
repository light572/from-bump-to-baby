OUR PURPOSE

Education that supports the journey.

From Bump to Baby is a mobile-first maternal and newborn education platform created around the educational vision of Nurse Kerubo Nyakundi.

What you will find

Pregnancy preparation, newborn care education, warning-sign awareness, first-aid awareness, parent checklists, a Mama Journal prototype and a route to ask about nurse consultations.

This platform provides general educational information. It does not diagnose conditions or replace individualized professional care.
