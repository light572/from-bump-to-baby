LEARN SAFELY

First-aid awareness

A webpage cannot replace practical training. Consider a recognised infant CPR and first-aid course.

Not breathing normally

Call emergency services immediately. If baby is unresponsive and not breathing normally, follow dispatcher instructions and your current infant CPR training.

Choking

If a baby cannot cough, cry or breathe effectively, get emergency help and use infant choking first aid as taught in a current recognised course.

Burns

Move away from the source safely and seek appropriate care. Do not apply ice, butter, toothpaste or household remedies.

Seizure

Protect the baby from nearby hazards. Do not restrain movements or put anything in the mouth. Seek urgent medical assessment.
