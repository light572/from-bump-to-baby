PART 5

Newborn First Aid

Build emergency awareness — and know when to get professional help.

Choking

If a baby cannot cough, cry or breathe effectively, call emergency services and use infant choking first aid as taught in a recognized current course. Never blindly sweep the mouth.

Not breathing normally

Call emergency services immediately. If the baby is unresponsive and not breathing normally, begin CPR according to dispatcher instructions and your current training.

Burns

Remove the baby from the source safely. Cool burns according to current first-aid guidance. Do not use ice, butter, toothpaste or household remedies.

Seizures

Protect the baby from hazards. Do not restrain movements or put anything in the mouth. Seek urgent medical assessment.

Learn hands-on

Infant CPR and choking response are practical skills. A recognized course is strongly recommended for parents and caregivers.
